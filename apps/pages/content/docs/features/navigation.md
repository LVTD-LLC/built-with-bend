# Explore the collection

Use the search field to find projects by name, description, or maker. Narrow results by category or discovery source, such as GitHub, X, or Reddit.

Open a project to find its live website, repository, and original source links. A repository is optional: closed-source projects belong here too.

Use the theme button to switch between light and dark. Your choice stays in this browser. The site works on phones and desktops.

## Share something you found

Choose [Submit a build](/submit/). All submissions are reviewed before publication. The [blog](/blog/) and these guides are public; no account or payment is required.
